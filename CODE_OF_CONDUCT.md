# Code of conduct

usagemeter follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the
work.

To report unacceptable behavior, open a
[private report](https://github.com/Elesiann/usagemeter/security/advisories/new)
or contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Elesiann).
Reports are handled confidentially.
